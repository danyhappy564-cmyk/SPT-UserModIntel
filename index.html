SPT-UserModIntel

Plain-language reviews of other people's mods for SPT (Single Player Tarkov). Each one explains what a mod actually does, its pros and cons, and whether it works with other mods.

  • Based on decompiling the mod DLLs and reading the code and setting descriptions directly.
  • Numbers such as FPS are measured only where a review says so.
